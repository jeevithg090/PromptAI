In the "To" field, add recipients. You can also add recipients in the "Cc" and "Bcc" fields. To hide a suggested recipient, right-click their profile photo, and click Hide suggestion. To find a hidden suggested recipient, enter their name, and click Show hidden suggestions.
Add a subject.
At the bottom of the message, click Send.
