The main areas to consider when writing good Gem instructions are the same as writing good prompts. You don't need to use all four, but using a few will help:
Persona Tell your Gem what role to play and how to respond.
Task Tell the Gem what you want Gemini to do or create.
Context Provide as much background as possible.
Format Be specific when specifying desired structure.
